Skills are installed into your LLM (Claude, Cursor, OpenCode, etc) to give you new slash commands.

- [Superpowers](https://github.com/obra/superpowers) gives you commands for the full product lifecycle, from brainstorming, planning, execution, and review, using TDD and worktrees for parallelism.
- [Superpowers (Ruby)](https://github.com/lucianghinda/superpowers-ruby) is a fork of the above, tuned for Ruby/Rails applications (so proper idioms, 37signals/Basecamp code style patterns, etc.)
- [Rails Upgrade Assistant](https://github.com/ombulabs/claude-code_rails-upgrade-skill) from Ombu Labs / FastRuby.io, who do this for a living.
